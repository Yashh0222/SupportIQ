# AcmeCRM Frequently Asked Questions

## What is AcmeCRM?
AcmeCRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management platform for small and mid-sized teams. It includes contact management, deal pipelines, email tracking, and reporting.

## What are the pricing plans?
AcmeCRM offers three plans:
- Starter: $19 per user per month
- Pro: $49 per user per month
- Enterprise: custom pricing (contact sales)

## How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to Settings -> Billing -> Plan, select your new plan, and confirm. The change takes effect immediately and the new price is prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle.

## Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. You can cancel from Settings -> Billing -> Cancel Subscription. Your account stays active until the end of the current billing period.

## What happens to my data after I cancel?
Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation, giving you time to export everything. After that, it is permanently deleted.

## How do I export my data?
Settings -> Data -> Export. You can export contacts, deals, and notes as CSV. Exports are emailed to the account owner within a few minutes.

## Does AcmeCRM support team collaboration?
Yes. Pro and Enterprise plans support multiple users with role-based permissions (admin, manager, and member roles).

## How do I add team members?
Settings -> Team -> Invite Member. Enter their email address and pick a role. They receive an invite link that expires in 48 hours.

## Can I import data from another CRM?
Yes. Use Settings -> Data -> Import. We support CSV imports for contacts and deals. The importer maps common column names automatically.
