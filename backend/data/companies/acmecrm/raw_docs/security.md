# AcmeCRM Security and Data Privacy

## Data encryption
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256. Backups are also encrypted.

## Data residency
By default, data is stored in the US-East region. Enterprise customers can choose EU or APAC data residency at no extra cost.

## Two-factor authentication
2FA is available on all plans. Enable it under Settings -> Security. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS codes.

## Single sign-on (SSO)
SSO with SAML 2.0 and Google Workspace is available on Enterprise plans. You can enforce SSO-only sign-in for your team.

## Access controls
Admins can set role-based permissions, restrict IP ranges for the whole workspace, and generate audit logs of who viewed or edited records.

## Data deletion
You can request full data deletion at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, and is completed within 30 days under the EU GDPR and California CCPA.

## Subprocessors
AcmeCRM uses Stripe for payments, AWS for hosting, and SendGrid for email delivery. A full subprocessor list is available on our privacy policy page.

## Reporting vulnerabilities
Found a security issue? Email security@acmecrm.com. We pay bug bounties for verified vulnerabilities and reply within 48 hours.
