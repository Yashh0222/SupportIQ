# AcmeCRM Billing and Invoices

## How billing works
Starter and Pro plans are billed monthly per active user. Enterprise plans are billed annually. You are only charged for active (non-archived) users.

## Payment methods
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and ACH for annual plans. All payments are processed by Stripe.

## Invoices
Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and emailed to the account owner. You can also view and download them any time under Settings -> Billing -> Invoices.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry automatically for 5 days. If it still fails, your account is downgraded to a read-only mode. Your data is never deleted due to a failed payment.

## Upgrading or downgrading
Plan changes are prorated. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. The number of users cannot exceed the plan limit, so downgrading may require you to archive or remove users first.

## Changing billing info
Update your credit card, billing email, or tax ID under Settings -> Billing -> Payment Method. Changes apply to the next invoice.

## Enterprise agreements
Enterprise customers receive a PDF agreement and a dedicated success manager. Annual contracts renew automatically unless you email sales@acmecrm.com at least 30 days before renewal.
