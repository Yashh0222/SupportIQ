# AcmeCRM Refund Policy

## Refund window
Every paid AcmeCRM plan comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied within the first 14 days of your initial purchase, you can request a full refund.

## How to request a refund
Email support@acmecrm.com with your account email and order ID. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and are returned to the original payment method.

## What is not refundable
- Renewal charges for Starter and Pro plans are refundable within 14 days of the renewal date only if no usage occurred during the renewal period.
- Enterprise contracts are billed annually and are non-refundable, but can be transferred to another team member.
- Third-party fees (payment processing, SMS credits, and integrations) are non-refundable.

## Partial refunds
If you upgraded mid-cycle and downgrade within 14 days, the difference is prorated and refunded. Setup and onboarding fees for Enterprise are non-refundable once services begin.

## Chargebacks
We do not block customers who file a chargeback, but repeated chargebacks will result in account suspension and referral to collections.

## Contact
For refund questions, email billing@acmecrm.com or chat with support in-app.
