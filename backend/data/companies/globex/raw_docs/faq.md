# Globex FAQ

## What is Globex?
Globex is an e-commerce and marketplace platform that lets online retailers
sell products across multiple channels from one dashboard.

## What are the available plans?
Globex offers three plans: Starter, Seller, and Marketplace. The Seller plan
adds multi-channel listings and inventory syncing. The Marketplace plan adds a
custom storefront.

## How do payouts work?
Seller and Marketplace plan payouts are processed every Friday. There is a
$25 minimum payout threshold.

## Do you support international selling?
Yes. Globex supports selling in 40 countries. Currency conversion fees apply
for sales made outside the store's base currency.
