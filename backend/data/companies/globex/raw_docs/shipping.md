# Globex Shipping & Delivery

Orders placed on Globex are shipped within 24 hours on business days.

- Standard delivery: 3–5 business days, free for Seller and Marketplace plans.
- Express delivery: 1–2 business days, $12 flat rate.
- International delivery: 7–14 business days, calculated at checkout.

Tracking links are sent by email once the order is picked up by the carrier.
