# Globex Refund Policy

Every paid Globex plan includes a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not
satisfied within the first 30 days of your initial purchase, you can request a
full refund.

Renewal charges are also refundable within 30 days of the renewal date, as long
as no usage occurred during the renewal period. Refund requests must be
submitted from the billing settings page; they are processed within 5 business
days.

Items that are not refundable: one-time onboarding fees and marketplace
listing deposits.
